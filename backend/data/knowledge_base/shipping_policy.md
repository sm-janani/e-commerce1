# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Timelines
Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days for metro cities and 7-10 business days for
other locations. Express delivery (1-3 business days) is available at checkout for an
additional fee in select pin codes.

## Shipping Charges
Orders above ₹999 qualify for free standard shipping. Orders below this amount incur a
flat shipping fee of ₹79. Express shipping is charged separately regardless of order
value.

## Tracking
A tracking number and carrier name are shared via email and SMS once an order is
shipped. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to become active on the carrier's website
after dispatch.

## Delayed or Lost Shipments
If a shipment shows no tracking movement for more than 5 days, customers should contact
support. A replacement or full refund is issued if the carrier confirms the shipment is
lost, with no cost to the customer.

## International Shipping
We currently ship only within India. International shipping is not supported at this
time.
