# Return & Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Items can be returned within 15 days of delivery if they are unused, in their original
packaging, and accompanied by the invoice. Innerwear, perishable goods, and personal
care items are not eligible for return due to hygiene reasons.

## Refund Timeline
Once a return is picked up and quality-checked at our warehouse, refunds are processed
within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders,
refunds are issued to the customer's bank account provided during the return request.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color are allowed within 15 days of delivery, subject
to stock availability. If the desired variant is unavailable, a refund will be issued
instead.

## Damaged or Wrong Item Received
If a customer receives a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, they should report it
within 48 hours of delivery with photos. In such cases, the return pickup and full
refund (including original shipping charges) are provided at no extra cost, and the
usual 15-day return window does not apply.

## Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge as long as they have not yet been shipped. Once
an order status changes to "shipped," it can no longer be cancelled but can be returned
after delivery under the standard return policy.
