# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I change my delivery address after placing an order?
Address changes are possible only before the order is shipped. Contact support with
your order ID as soon as possible and we will update it if the order has not yet left
the warehouse.

## Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes, COD is available for orders under ₹10,000 in most serviceable pin codes. A small
COD handling fee of ₹49 may apply.

## How can I apply a coupon code?
Coupon codes can be entered at checkout in the "Apply Coupon" field before payment.
Only one coupon can be applied per order, and coupons cannot be applied retroactively
to already-placed orders.

## What payment methods are accepted?
We accept credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, popular wallets, and Cash on Delivery.

## How do I contact a human support agent?
You can ask this assistant to connect you with a human agent at any time, or email
support@example-store.com. Complex billing disputes, fraud concerns, and legal
requests are always routed to a human agent.

## Is gift wrapping available?
Gift wrapping is available for ₹49 per item and can be selected at checkout.

## My payment was deducted but the order shows as failed. What should I do?
This usually resolves automatically within 5-7 business days as the bank reverses the
amount. If it does not reverse within that window, contact support with the payment
reference ID for manual verification.
